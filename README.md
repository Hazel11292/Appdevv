My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

About the Movie

My favorite movie is Spider-Man. It is a superhero movie about a young man named Peter Parker who gets special powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

Why I Like It

- The action scenes are exciting.
- Spider-Man is funny and brave.
- The story is interesting.
- It teaches us to help other people.

My Favorite Character

Peter Parker (Spider-Man)

I like Peter Parker because he is brave, helpful, and responsible even when he faces difficult problems.

My Favorite Movie Quote

«With great power comes great responsibility.»

My Movie Rating

Category| Rating
Story| 10/10
Action| 10/10
Characters| 10/10
Entertainment| 10/10

My Favorite Things About Spider-Man

1. His web-slinging abilities.
2. His superhero costume.
3. His bravery in saving people.
4. His funny personality.

Conclusion

I recommend Spider-Man because it is entertaining and teaches an important lesson about responsibility. It is one of my favorite superhero movies.
